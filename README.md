# Vendor Sales & Profit Analytics Dashboard — Power BI

## Project Overview

The **Vendor Sales & Profit Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, vendor contribution, brand performance, and inventory behavior.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data from Excel/CSV sources into meaningful business insights that can help organizations make better decisions around **pricing, vendor management, inventory planning, and profitability optimization**.

---

## Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall revenue and profitability
- Identify high-performing and low-performing brands
- Compare vendor-level sales and profit contribution
- Understand the relationship between sales and inventory
- Analyze monthly revenue and profit trends
- Identify opportunities for improving profit margins
- Support data-driven vendor and inventory decisions

## Dashboard Features

### 1.Brand Performance Analysis

The dashboard compares profitability across different brands to identify:

- Top-performing brands
- Low-performing brands
- Brands contributing significantly to overall profit
- Differences in profitability across product categories

### 2.Vendor Analysis

Vendor-level analysis provides insights into:

- Revenue generated by each vendor
- Profit contribution by vendor
- Vendor performance comparison
- Identification of high-value vendors

This analysis can support better **vendor selection and purchasing decisions**.

### 3. Profitability Analysis

The dashboard evaluates:

- Revenue
- Cost
- Profit
- Profit margin
- Brand-level profitability
- Impact of purchasing quantities on margins

This helps identify potential opportunities to improve profitability through better pricing and purchasing strategies.

### 4.Sales & Inventory Analysis

The dashboard analyzes the relationship between:

- Units sold
- Inventory levels
- Sales performance

The analysis can help identify potential **overstocking and understocking situations**, supporting better inventory planning.

### 5.Time-Based Sales Trends

Monthly trend analysis is used to understand:

- Revenue trends
- Profit trends
- Sales fluctuations
- Seasonal patterns
- Changes in business performance over time

## Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis:

- **Brand performance varies significantly**, with certain brands contributing substantially more profit than others.
- **Vendor performance differs across revenue and profitability**, highlighting opportunities to optimize vendor selection.
- **Bulk purchasing quantities show variation in profit margins**, suggesting differences in pricing, purchasing costs, or product-level profitability.

## Key Calculations

### Profit

```DAX
Total Profit =
SUM('Sales'[Revenue]) - SUM('Sales'[Cost])
```

### Total Revenue

```DAX
Total Revenue =
SUM('Sales'[Revenue])
```

### Total Units Sold

```DAX
Total Units Sold =
SUM('Sales'[Units Sold])
```

### Profit Margin

```DAX
Profit Margin =
DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Revenue], 0)
```

> Replace `Sales` and the column names with the actual names used in your Power BI model.
